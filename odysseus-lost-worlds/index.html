How the ranking works

Only completed journeys qualify. Regular enemies: 100 points each (clones and boss life carriers give no points). Bosses: 1,200 each. Stage progress + homecoming: 10,000. Speed bonus: 2 points per second saved under 60 minutes. Defense bonus: up to 3,000, minus 5 per HP of damage taken. Each retry costs 500 points. After a retry, enemies keep their wounds and stay defeated; each kill counts once.

All difficulties currently share one ranking; scores are not separated by difficulty. Higher scores rank first. Ties go to the faster journey, then the earlier submission. Shared energy stores 2 bars. Q — Athena (base 145 directional damage), W — Achilles (base 210 in a narrow forward spear rush), and E — Hermes (base 180 nearby) each cost 1 bar. R — Zeus deals base 125 damage to all visible enemies and costs 2 bars. All attacks gain +8% of base damage per stage after the first battle stage. Normal attacks restore 4% of actual damage dealt as HP, rising to 10% during the final Antinous battle. Athena delivers the final blow to Antinous.
